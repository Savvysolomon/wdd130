Akwa Ibom

Akwa Ibom State is a major oil-producing coastal state in the south-south region of Nigeria, created on September 23, 1987, from the former Cross River State. It's capital city is Uyo. Akwa Ibom borders Rivers and Abia states to the west, Cross River State to the east, and the Atlantic Ocean to the south. It has 31 administrative regions (Local Government Areas) and an estimated population of over 5 to 7 million residents.
