Unforgettable River Adventures

Welcome to White Water Rafting Company, where adrenaline meets the untamed beauty of the great outdoors. Our mission is to deliver unforgettable, high-energy river adventures that connect thrill-seekers, families, and nature lovers with the world's most spectacular waterways. We provide tailored, fully guided excursions designed to push your boundaries and create stories that last a lifetime.

Expert Standards & Rapid Tiers

We elevate your river experience by combining world-class safety with personalized adventure. We proudly feature a flexible rapid tier system, offering everything from gentle Class I family floats to heart-pounding Class IV expeditions. Your peace of mind is our absolute priority, which is why we invest in state-of-the-art safety gear, premium self-bailing rafts, and certified swiftwater rescue guides.

History

Founded in 2014 by a group of lifelong river guides, White Water Rafting began with just two secondhand rafts and a passion for sharing the untamed currents of White River Canyon.

Today, we are proud to have safely guided thousands of thrill-seekers down the rapids, turning first-time paddlers into lifelong river enthusiasts.
