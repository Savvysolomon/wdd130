About Us - White Water Rafting

At White Water Rafting, our purpose is to reconnect people with the raw, untamed beauty of nature through unforgettable river experiences, breaking guests out of their daily routines to challenge their boundaries and foster a lifelong respect for our planet's wild waterways.

History

Founded in 2014 by a group of lifelong river guides, White Water Rafting began with just two secondhand rafts and a passion for sharing the untamed currents of White River Canyon.

Today, we are proud to have safely guided thousands of thrill-seekers down the rapids, turning first-time paddlers into lifelong river enthusiasts.
